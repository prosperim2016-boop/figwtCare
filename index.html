ABOUT US

Connecting Organisations With Suitable Care Professionals

At FiGwt Care, we aim to make finding suitable care professionals simpler for organisations within the health and social care sector.

We connect care providers with reliable, compassionate and suitably experienced care professionals based on their staffing requirements.

We believe that effective staffing should be based on professionalism, experience, reliability, dignity and respect.

Important Information

FiGwt Care provides staffing and introductory services connecting care providers with care professionals.

Depending on the service provided and the arrangements agreed with the relevant organisation, care professionals may be introduced for potential employment, engagement or other appropriate working arrangements.

Organisations remain responsible for their own recruitment decisions, employment or engagement arrangements, and any required checks, assessments and ongoing supervision.
